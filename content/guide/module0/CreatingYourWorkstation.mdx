# Creating Your Workstation

When working in Fusion360, it's best to keep everything organized to avoid any confusion later. This is done through having projects, nested folders, and files, all appropriately named. Projects are essentially top-level folders and should be reserved for large designs, like FTC robot assemblies or separate long-term builds, like our lantern-fly traps. Folders should be used to organize different parts of the project. For example, for our robot, we have different folders for different parts of the robot: the intake, outtake, and transfer are all in separate folders.
