# Creating Your Workstation

When working in Fusion360, it's best to keep everything organized to avoid any confusion later. This is done through having projects, nested folders, and files, all appropriately named.

**Projects** are essentially top-level folders and should be reserved for large designs, like FTC robot assemblies or separate long-term builds, like our lantern-fly traps. 

**Folders** are used to organize different parts of a project. For example, for our robot, we have different folders for different parts of the robot: the intake, outtake, and transfer are all in separate folders. 

**Files** are the actual designs themselves. 

To make your first project, click the "New Project" button in the top left of the "All Projects" page and type the name of your first project. We suggest calling it "JellyCAD". 

Then, inside "JellyCAD", click the "New Folder" button and call your new folder "Module 0". We will be making new folders for every module.

Making new files is as easy as pressing the + button at the top of the screen, similar to adding new tabs in a browser. Once you've made a new file, save the file by hitting "Ctrl + S" or going to "File > Save As". You can call this file "Tour". **Make sure to save it to your new Module 0 folder!**
