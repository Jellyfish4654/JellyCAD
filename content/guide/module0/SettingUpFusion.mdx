# Setting Up Fusion360

Here's how to set up Fusion360:

1. Sign in to the  Autodesk Fusion360 website at < https://www.autodesk.com/ > - the sign in button should be in the top right. Make sure to use your school email address.

2. Press Products > Top Products > Fusion

3. Click Access for free

4. Click Access Education Subscription

5. Click "student"

6. Fill in all the information and press submit

7. Download Fusion360 for free

8. Sign in Again in browser once the download is finishsed

9. Select "yes"

10. Create a hub - the name doesn't matter

11. Go to your new hub

12. Click "Create a design"

You're all set!
