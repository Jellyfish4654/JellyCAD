# Navigating A File

Here are some ways to change your view within a file. 

**View Cube**: Dragging on the ViewCube in the top right allows you to orbit your design, viewing it from different perspectives. Clicking on a face will send you to that predefined view. You can also click on the arrows surrounding the view cube to rotate positions easily. 

![TourFiles](/assets/TourViewCube.gif)
<small>Using the ViewCube.</small>

> [!NOTE]
>
> These three next tools are located next to each other in the navigation bar at the bottom.

**Pan**: Clicking the pan tool (looks like a hand) allows you to move two-dimensionally without rotating. 

![TourFiles](/assets/TourPan.gif)
<small>Using the Pan tool.</small>

**Zoom**: You can zoom in and out, either using the scroll wheel on a mouse or by dragging two fingers together on a track pad.

![TourFiles](/assets/TourZoom.gif)
<small>Zooming in and out.</small>

**Free Orbit**: Using free orbit allows you to rotate more freely than the view cube. 

![TourFiles](/assets/TourFreeOrbit.gif)
<small>Using the Free Orbit tool.</small>
