# Introduction To Modeling

In this lesson, you'll get an overview of the process of modeling 3D objects by creating a cube. 

First, create a new folder within the JellyCAD Project called "Module 1". Then, make a new file called "Intro to Modeling" and save it to Module 1.

![TourFiles](/assets/NewFile1.1.gif)
<small>Creating a new folder for Module 1 and a file within it.</small>

>[!WARNING]
>
> This is the last time we'll show how to create new folders and files!

When modeling 3D objects, we begin by creating sketches, which we then extrude to make 3D. To create your first sketch, hit the "Create Sketch" button in the Toolbar. Next, click the plane to create your sketch on. We suggest creating your sketch on the plane that aligns with the "Top" on the ViewCube.

Our sketch will become the bottom face of a cube, 20mm by 20mm. Click on the rectangle tool. 

>[!NOTE]
>
> By default, the rectangle tool requires 2 points that are the corners of the rectangle. 

Place your first corner at the origin, then drag towards the top-right. To input the dimensions of the rectangle, type 20 into the first box - that will be the height. Hit "tab" to switch to the next box to specify the width, press enter, and now we have a sketch! Hit "Finish Sketch" once you are done. 

![TourFiles](/assets/RectangleSketch1.1.gif)
<small>Creating a sketch of a 20mm by 20mm rectangle.</small>

To turn our sketch into a 3D object, we must extrude it. Click the "Extrude" button in the Toolbar, and select the rectangle we've just made. Specify the amount of extrusion - 20mm - and hit enter. 

![TourFiles](/assets/ExtrudeRectangle1.1.gif)
<small>Extruding our rectangle sketch to create a cube!</small>

Now we have a cube!
